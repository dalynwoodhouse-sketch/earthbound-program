The Story

“There’s More Than One Way To Soar”

PROLOGUE

There is a magical place called Avalon that runs parallel to our own, hidden from all, save those who know where to look. Every fifty years new faeries are born in Avalon. Many years ago, a faerie named Adaire was born without wings. Shortly after Adaire sprouted, Nyx, the evil Kelpie Queen approached the lonely faerie with a seemingly harmless offer. She offered to give Adaire wings in exchange for her hidden power. When Adaire would reach maturity, she would have complete command over the land. She was destined to remain earthbound to be the land’s guardian and caretaker. Little Adaire felt she had nothing left to lose, and so she took the deal. Nyx, true to their bargain, ripped Adaire’s powers from her body and bestowed upon her wings. But they were not at all what Adaire had hoped for. Her dark wings would always set her apart from her kin.

Fifty years passed by, and Nyx’s dark heart eventually corrupted the stolen power. That corruption bled into the earth, slowly destroying Avalon.

ACT I

Spring is here. Rain helps the lovely flowers bloom, and a rainbow lights the sky. All of Avalon prepares for the arrival of the new baby faeries. The Swan Princess and her flock gather magical flowers to gift to the Fae at the Festival of Life. Adaire longs for a little faerie of her own. She is haunted by the choices she made as a young faerie. Bree, the midwife Faerie, hosts the festivities and greets the soon to be mothers. Adaire arrives alone. The Elven Huntress from the North and Elissa, the Swan Princess are greeted warmly by all. Six little faeries are born. Adaire is heartbroken that there is no little faerie for her to care for. Avalon celebrates together, save Adaire, who looks on devastated. Everyone leaves at the close of the festival, but Adaire lingers to express her sorrow to Bree.

Little Rowan, the wingless faerie, suddenly sprouts. Adaire is shocked and delighted. She has a little faerie of her own but she and the midwife worry over Rowan’s lack of wings. Little Rowan is unbothered. She loves herself and is grateful for the budding power she has within her. Little Rowan’s budding power over the land attracts Nyx’s attention. She tries to entice Rowan into giving up her power in exchange for wings, just like her mother did. Bree and Adaire quickly decide to hide Rowan away in the human world for her safety, giving her powers time to develop. Bree distracts Nyx, giving Adaire and Rowan a chance to get away. The two faeries find temporary sanctuary amongst a flock of birds. A little bird named Melody, bravely volunteers to accompany Rowan into the human world. Adaire asks Eveline, the human Queen, to take Rowan as her ward. Adaire watches Rowan go, wishing there was another way to keep her safe.

ACT II

Ten years pass by. Rowan grows up in the human world with Melody by her side. Her powers begin to manifest. Rowan and Melody spend most of their time frolicking about in the woods playing with the squirrels, instead of remaining in the stuffy confines of the palace. Queen Eveline calls them in from their play to attend a parade. The townspeople have gathered to enjoy the entertainment of the royal party. Acrobats and jesters perform for the royalty, the court children, and the townspeople. Rowan is beloved by all, including her four adopted princess sisters. She is well known in town for her bravery, confidence, and kindness.

Nyx arrives in disguise, pretending to be part of the entertainment. She spins a tale of a young maiden who was distraught and depressed. Nothing would appease her, save the gift of glorious wings. The townspeople look on transfixed, unaware Nyx is trying to tempt Rowan, while mocking her mother Adaire. Luckily, Melody sees through Nyx’s disguise and flies off to find Adaire. Just as Rowan reaches out to touch Nyx’s magical wings, Melody swoops in to pull her back to safety.

Adaire and Rowan are reunited once more. Rowan says goodbye to her human family and thanks them for keeping her safe these past years. Adaire, Rowan, and Melody are guided back to Avalon with the help of the Wind and the keepers of Avalon, the Druids.

ACT III

Adaire, Rowan, and Melody arrive safely in Avalon. Just as they are arriving, they discover some mischievous Leprechauns. At first, the Leprechauns tease Rowan for not having any wings. Melody tries to defend Rowan, but Rowan pulls the brave little bird away. Rowan loves the Leprechauns freely and soon wins them over. The three faeries seek out the Elven Huntress, in the hopes that she and her elves will teach them to fight.

The Kelpies separate Rowan from the rest of her party. They create an illusion to convince Rowan that the other faeries will never accept her if she doesn’t have wings. The Kelpie Queen arrives and makes a final attempt to entice Rowan into a deal. Rowan refuses. A line is drawn and the troops assemble. A battle of good versus evil begins. Adaire is struck down in battle, protecting Rowan. Rowan rises up and defeats the Kelpie Queen in a clash of their magical powers. She takes Adaire’s power back leaving Nyx utterly ruined.

The warriors mourn Adaire. They pay respect by placing the magical flowers of Avalon over her body. Adaire stirs and rises once more. Inspired by Rowan’s bravery to love herself as she is, Adaire gives up her wings and asks Rowan for the power Nyx took from her. Adaire and Rowan heal the blackened land together. All of Avalon celebrates their triumph!
